Why it fails outdoors: the training images are single detached leaves photographed face-up on a uniform background. A network can score well on them without learning much about disease — one published study reached 49% using nothing but 8 background pixels. Field photographs have soil, sky, hands and uneven light, and the accuracy collapses. Studio benchmarks are not evidence that a model works. The model served here is statically quantized to int8: 3.5× smaller than the float version and, on a laptop CPU, slightly faster — where the one-line dynamic quantization was 2.2× slower and could not run in a browser at all.
